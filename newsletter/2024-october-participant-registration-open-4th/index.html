🌟 4th Applied Active Inference Symposium - November 13-15, 2024 🌟

Join us for an exciting exploration of Active Inference across various domains and scales! This free, online event welcomes participants from all backgrounds.

🎤 Opening Keynote: Karl Friston 📣 Presenters and Workshop facilitators (alphabetical): Alex Kiefer, Alex Schmidt, Alexey Tolchinsky, Andrew Pashea, Aswin Paul, Bert Berkers, Bradly Alicea, Devin Bae, Denise Holt, Giacomo Rossettini, Ines Hipolito, Jean-François Cloutier, John Boik, John H Clippinger, , Laura Desiree Di Paolo, Mahault Albarracin, Matthew Brown, Mehran Hossein Zadeh Bazargani, Michael Walters, Octopus, Parvin Malekzadeh, Rafael Kaufmann, Shanna Dobson, Sonia de Jager, Viet Dung Nguyen, William Gebhardt.... and more to be added....

🎯 Highlights:

  1. Broad range of projects across 25+ presenters, ranging from sustainability and urban planning to biologically plausible decision-making models
  2. Interactive workshops and panels, including a session on open-source strategies
  3. Connecting with useful materials (educational, code, etc) and people who are learning and applying Active Inference

🔔 Register now to stay updated on additional interactive events and workshops being added over the next month!
