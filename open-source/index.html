The default license for Institute materials — including software repositories, research outputs, and other public work products — is Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Specific products and collaborations may use different terms; check the individual repository or resource for details.

Default License

CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 is the default license for Institute materials. It lets others distribute, remix, adapt, and build on the material in any medium or format, for noncommercial purposes, as long as they credit the creator and license any adaptations under the same terms.

GitHub organizationProjects

What the License Requires

Attribution (BY): credit must be given to the creator(s) of the work and, where known, the Institute as the hosting or publishing entity. NonCommercial (NC): only noncommercial use is permitted — use not primarily intended for or directed toward commercial advantage or monetary compensation. ShareAlike (SA): adaptations must be shared under the same license terms, unless otherwise specified and agreed upon by the creators.

Open Source Repositories

The Institute publishes software across repositories hosted in the Active Inference Institute GitHub organization. These include Biofirm (Active Inference PyMDP agents for bioregional modeling), GEN24 (generative AI experiments under Active Blockference), ActiveInferAnts (Active Inference ant simulations), Journal-Utilities and ActiveInferenceJournal (supporting the Active Inference Journal), ActiveInferenceCategoryTheory (category theory curriculum), ActiveBlockference (integrating Active Inference with cadCAD), Active_Inference_Ontology and AEOS (ontology snapshots), GeneralizedNotationNotation, the Parr et al. 2022 Active Inference Textbook repository, Symposium (methods for the Applied Active Inference Symposium), and CEREBRUM. Each repository sets its own license, and most differ from the Institute's CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 default: GeneralizedNotationNotation carries the default CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms; Biofirm, GEN24, ActiveBlockference, and Symposium are MIT licensed; ActiveInferAnts and CEREBRUM are CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, which does not permit derivatives or adaptations; ActiveInferenceJournal, Active_Inference_Ontology, and the Parr et al. 2022 Active Inference Textbook repository are CC BY 4.0, with no NonCommercial or ShareAlike restriction; and Journal-Utilities, ActiveInferenceCategoryTheory, and AEOS are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. Always check a repository's own LICENSE file before reuse.

Community contributions are welcome on most repositories; check each repository for its own contribution guidelines.

Institute Programs turn interest into participation through volunteer, internship, mentorship, fellowship, partnership, philanthropy, grants, and open-source pathways. All programs are open to people of any background and familiarity level.

 Researchers, modelers, engineers, and contributors building Active Inference software, tools, and scientific knowledge. ReInference Unit

ReInference is the Institute's research and development arm. It hosts software projects, theoretical work, applied modeling, geospatial analysis, knowledge engineering, and interdisciplinary research under the Active Inference framework. ReInference projects range from widely-used open-source tools to specialized research initiatives.
