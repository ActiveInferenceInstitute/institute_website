This is a volunteer position that is designed to be valuable, meaningful, and time-efficient for SAB members as well as the Institute.

SAB members with different skills and perspectives (including a wide range of familiarity with Active Inference) engage with the Active Inference Ecosystem in multiple ways including:

  • Join optional monthly SAB discussion sessions to give insights & connect with other SAB members.
  • Support the Institute by making connections and collaborating on philanthropic/grant programs.
  • Participate directly in ongoing Institute activities, and propose/lead education or research projects of their own.
  • Lead alternative forms of engagement — suggest your own idea for SAB contribution!

See here for more information on the SAB: https://www.activeinference.org/about/scientific-advisory-board

Here is the direct link to the form to apply for the SAB. Please submit your application for the 2024 SAB before December 15, 2023.

Let us know here, or by email [Contact the Institute](site-contact) if you have any questions.
