Grupo de Textos Escolares

Aprendizaje por cohortes estructurado a través de libros de texto de Active Inference — 9 cohortes en el libro de texto de 2022, ahora disponibles en la primera cohorte de Fundamentos 2026.

Liderar: Daniel Friedman

Textbook GroupRegister

El Grupo de Libros de Texto es un programa educativo del Instituto sostenido que ofrece aprendizaje basado en cohortes estructurado a través de libros de texto de Active Inference. Desde 2022 ha realizado 9 cohortes con el libro de texto de 2022 'Active Inference: The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, and Behavior' de Thomas Parr, Giovanni Pezzulo y Karl J. Friston. A mediados de 2026, el grupo está activo en su primera cohorte con el libro de texto de 2026 'Fundamentals of Active Inference: Principles, Algorithms, and Applications of the Free Energy Principle for Engineers' de Sanjeev V. Namjoshi.

Descripción general

The Textbook Group brings participants together in structured cohorts to work through Active Inference textbooks with guidance and peer support. Each cohort runs regular sessions with discussion, exercises, and collaborative work through key texts.

The project is coordinated by Daniel Friedman.

Cohortes actuales y pasadas

Since 2022 the Textbook Group has completed 9 cohorts on the 2022 textbook 'Active Inference: The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, and Behavior' (Parr, Pezzulo & Friston). As of mid-2026 it is live in its first cohort on the 2026 textbook 'Fundamentals of Active Inference: Principles, Algorithms, and Applications of the Free Energy Principle for Engineers' (Namjoshi).

Recordings and materials from prior cohorts are accessible through the repository and course pages.
