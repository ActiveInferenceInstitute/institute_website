Permite a otros distribuir, remixar, adaptar y construir sobre el material en cualquier medio o formato, con fines no comerciales, siempre y cuando reconozcan al creador y licencien las adaptaciones bajo los mismos términos.

Repositorios de código abierto

The Institute publishes software across repositories hosted in the Active Inference Institute GitHub organization. These include Biofirm (Active Inference PyMDP agents for bioregional modeling), GEN24 (generative AI experiments under Active Blockference), ActiveInferAnts (Active Inference ant simulations), Journal-Utilities and ActiveInferenceJournal (supporting the Active Inference Journal), ActiveInferenceCategoryTheory (category theory curriculum), ActiveBlockference (integrating Active Inference with cadCAD), Active_Inference_Ontology and AEOS (ontology snapshots), GeneralizedNotationNotation, the Parr et al. 2022 Active Inference Textbook repository, Symposium (methods for the Applied Active Inference Symposium), and CEREBRUM.

Each repository sets its own license, and most differ from the Institute's CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 default: GeneralizedNotationNotation carries the default CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms; Biofirm, GEN24, ActiveBlockference, and Symposium are MIT licensed; ActiveInferAnts and CEREBRUM are CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, which does not permit derivatives or adaptations; ActiveInferenceJournal, Active_Inference_Ontology, and the Parr et al. 2022 Active Inference Textbook repository are CC BY 4.0, with no NonCommercial or ShareAlike restriction; and Journal-Utilities, ActiveInferenceCategoryTheory, and AEOS are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. Always check a repository's own LICENSE file before reuse.

Las contribuciones a estos repositorios provienen a través de las vías de participación del Instituto: voluntariado, pasantía y becas incluyen trabajo de software y documentación en proyectos de código abierto.
