Updates from the Active Inference Ecosystem

  • cpomdp project (Dan Corva) reports a new publication “State-Dependent Observation Noise Reintroduces Epistemic Value in Linear-Gaussian Active Inference” (arXiv:2607.20306). cpomdp is a JAX-native continuous-state active inference toolbox, the continuous-state sibling to pymdp. One of its routines compiles a specified agent down to a fixed linear-Gaussian filter wherever that reduction is valid. For models with state-dependent observation noise, R(x), it wouldn’t compile. Rather than patch around it, Corva chased the obstruction and found it was structural: no fixed linear-Gaussian filter can reproduce these agents at all. That became a typed IncompatibleLinearizationError in cpomdp v0.4, and now a preprint .
  • Precious Adeniyi working on the PVF-U (Primitive Valence Foundation—Unified) project reports on a three-tiered architecture for multi-agent AI systems. The project addressed the Emptiness Asymptote—a failure mode where agents freeze in predictable environments without social partners.
  • Artificial Sentience project (Cleber Gomes) reports a new blog post: Dissociating Perception and Reality: A Predictive Coding Framework for State Estimation, Synaptic Plasticity, and Emotional Valence Under Non-Optimal Generative Weights. This post explores the epistemological and mechanical boundaries between physical ground-truth reality and an agent’s internal subjective reconstruction.
  • Daniel Friedman reports a new publication and codebase on “Active Inference Power Suite: Conditional Statistical Power under Controlled Generative Settings”. The suite combines fixed-horizon procedures, analytic references, dependence/calibration experiments, and a discrete-state active-inference agent to explore topics related to statistical power.
