Coherence Density

Measuring internal coordination in complex adaptive systems.

Lead: Travis James

Ecosystem

Coherence Density is an exploratory Ecosystem research project establishing a measurement-focused framework to quantify internal coordination, stability, and information flow in complex adaptive systems under Active Inference.

概要

Coherence Density develops testable indicators and formal relationships to evaluate how internal organization and coordination are maintained while systems minimize free energy and adapt to uncertainty.
