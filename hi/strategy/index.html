Vision and Mission

Vision. A world where active inference — a framework of adaptive behavior grounded in first principles — is known, taught, and applied across science, engineering, and everyday life.

Mission. To translate the science of how natural systems adapt and self-organize, known as active inference, into practical tools, resources, and educational experiences that empower people, communities, and technology to enhance decision making and reduce uncertainty.

Our Values and Principles

We are committed to fostering a culture of excellence, collaboration, and innovation. Our values and principles serve as the guiding principles that shape our Strategy and define our organization's character.

  • Active Inference and Exploration. We embrace the principles of Active Inference, a Systems Approach, and open-ended exploration as fundamental driving forces. We cultivate a culture of curiosity and continuous learning. Through endeavors across multiple scales — person, project, Institute, Ecosystem — we enrich our understanding and make relevant contributions to our niche.
  • Integrity. We uphold and promote honesty, accountability, professionalism, and responsible conduct in research, education, and facilitation among members of the Institute, the Ecosystem, and the communities we serve. We foster diversity, respect, and global inclusion through community engagement. We treat differences in perspective and understanding as a wellspring of creative and productive potential, driving breakthroughs and strengthening collaborative research outcomes.
  • Institute- and Ecosystem-Scale Generative Modeling. We use model-based approaches to organizational design and operation. Informally we use the Active Inference Ontology where possible, looking towards more sophisticated computational modeling in the future. We support shared informational niches across scales — Ecosystem, Institute, Organizational Units, Projects — and continuously develop and refine hierarchical models, drawing on sensory information, exploiting data, and gathering feedback. Our dynamic self-modeling enables efficient resource allocation.
  • Anticipatory Behavior. Our commitment to anticipatory behavior equips us to excel in uncertain environments. Leveraging our internal models, we generate predictions at various scales and time horizons, empowering us to take initiative and adapt our policies accordingly — planning strategically, making informed decisions, and remaining at the forefront of our fields.
  • Continuous Development. Embodying open-endedness and techno-evolution, we embrace continuous development. Recognizing the dynamic nature of our environment and constant advancements in science and technology, we continually evolve our internal models and approaches. In the spirit of action and perception, we encourage learners to produce and share artifacts, then receive feedback: informally and formally (through Prepare and Measure).
  • Participatory Engagement. We encourage collaborative active learning through artifacts. Digital, stigmergic modifications of our online environment are the central method for engagement. Projects are enacted through preparation and periodic measurements that trace development through time. We support the accessibility, applicability, and inclusivity of Active Inference by seeking Open Source (and related: Open Science, DeSci) approaches where possible.

शिक्षा

At the participant scale, we approach a participant's Active Inference mastery as a system of interest. To reflect a plurality of individual priors, expertise levels, and preferences, we develop different educational formats — workshops, discussion groups, online courses, project-based learning, internships, and more — and our preference is to have up to a thousand participants involved in education.

At the Institute scale, the organization learns state-of-the-art ways of working and practices across functional domains to build and strengthen organizational capabilities. At the ecosystem scale, the Institute works on education and on reducing research debt for a broad community, developing public video and audio content to spread Active Inference and aiming to reach tens of thousands of people with it.

अनुसंधान

At the participant scale, we provide affordances to scaffold and launch research and development projects by participants; projects can be driven by participants inside or outside the organization, and the Institute provides relevant support, with the expectation of dozens of research outcomes such as papers and workshop and conference presentations. At the Institute scale, the organization arranges, catalyzes, and participates in Active Inference research projects with long-term impact or public-goods features — such as ontology development and knowledge engineering — and events like symposiums and conferences.

At the ecosystem scale, we recognize and seek to carry out, apply, and improve upon research practices from open science.

आउटरीच और जुड़ाव

At the participant scale, we think of participants as ambassadors of the Institute, and we welcome and support their initiatives to share a voice about Active Inference across situations and communities. At the Institute scale, we aim to set up partnerships with other organizations from academia, industry, and beyond, supporting high-quality science communication, education, and collaboration around research topics such as Active Inference.

At the ecosystem scale, we acknowledge the diversity of people's initial conditions — familiarity with language, science, philosophy, and computation — and we develop the Active Inference online journal and create popular-science content for different social media.

तरीके

At the participant scale, we educate participants so they grow their expertise across disciplines; because disciplinary expertise entails competency in practices and tools, we take a holistic approach to participant professional development. At the Institute scale, continuous organizational evolution is our fundamental effort — the Institute seeks to obtain and use the most advanced expertise in all practices, applying Active Inference, Systems Thinking, and other first-principles approaches to its own operations.

At the ecosystem scale, we share and promote the methods we use through the community to increase the quality and efficiency of collaborations, and we remain open to recommendations for new methods and strategies to consider.
