2026 is the Active Inference Institute's ongoing year — a consolidated, month-by-month entry point to the activities, programs, and events that are running across the organization under the motto Act. Infer. Serve., in the same format as the 2025 annual overview. The Activities page and Calendar remain the most reliable way to see what's happening week to week. The detailed record continues to be maintained on the 2026 hub.

The Year So Far

2026 is in progress: two of four Quarterly Roundtables have livestreamed (March 27 and June 26), the call for presenters is open for the 6th Applied Active Inference Symposium (November 12–14, 2026), and the year opened with Institute Structure clarification alongside the new Board of Directors and Scientific Advisory Board. This page grows month by month as the year unfolds — the Activities page and Calendar are the most current view. For the completed prior year, see the 2025 annual overview.
