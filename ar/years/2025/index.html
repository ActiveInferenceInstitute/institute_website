2025 was the Active Inference Institute's fifth full year: four Quarterly Roundtables, the 5th Applied Active Inference Symposium (November 12–14), the Theoretical Neurobiology Group joining the Institute, a new RxInfer.jl partnership, completion of the Project ~ Measurement system, and selection of the 2026 Board — a completed year in review, organized month by month. The detailed record continues to be maintained on the 2025 hub.

A Year in Review

2025 closed with the Institute's fifth Applied Active Inference Symposium and a completed governance cycle. This retrospective maps the year month by month: new groups hosted (Theoretical Neurobiology), a new partnership (RxInfer.jl Learning Group), four Quarterly Roundtables, the Project ~ Measurement system, and the selection of 2026 leadership. For the Institute's longer arc, see the History page; for the year that followed, see 2026.

Anyone who wants to understand where the Institute came from and how it grew. التاريخ

The Institute's history runs from a 2020 co-founder team meeting around a shared interest in Active Inference, through the Active Inference Lab, to the nonprofit Active Inference Institute it is today. This page traces that story year by year. For the current and upcoming years, see the 2025 and 2026 annual overviews.

 Members, participants, collaborators, and the public who want a consolidated, month-by-month view of the Institute's ongoing year in 2026. 2026

2026 is the Active Inference Institute's ongoing year — a consolidated, month-by-month entry point to the activities, programs, and events that are running across the organization under the motto Act. Infer. Serve., in the same format as the 2025 annual overview. The Activities page and Calendar remain the most reliable way to see what's happening week to week.
