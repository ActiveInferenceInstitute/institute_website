Active inference through completion of the softmax formula and extension using Bannach Contractions, Fixed-Point arithmetic, and sparse-max.

An independent proposal to examine softmax alternatives, fixed-point arithmetic and sparse-max in Active Inference-related architectures.

Discord invite

Daniel Dragolich proposes an independent systems-engineering investigation of alternatives and extensions to softmax, including contraction mappings, fixed-point arithmetic and sparse-max. The proposer seeks hardware and firmware contributors and independent examination of available code and artifacts. The immediate objective is a reproducible test protocol and critical review; theoretical claims, proof validity and performance remain to be evaluated. Listing invites voluntary collaboration and does not validate the claims or establish Institute funding or a research partnership.

Participate

Discuss the proposed scope, review available artifacts and agree on a bounded first milestone with the project facilitator. Progress and open questions can be shared through the Institute's project forms.
