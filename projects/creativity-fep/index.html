This Ecosystem project develops accounts of creativity and creative agents under the Free Energy Principle — examining how generative models and free energy minimization explain, predict, and enhance creative behavior.

Overview

The project designs and researches creativity as an Active Inference process — asking how creative agents update beliefs, explore possibility spaces, and produce novel outputs. The work connects theoretical accounts with practical research on creative practice. The project is led by Héctor Manrique, a member of the Institute's Scientific Advisory Board.
