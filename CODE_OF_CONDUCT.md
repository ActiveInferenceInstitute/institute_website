# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

The Active Inference Institute is committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and
constructive environment for everyone who contributes to this repository —
regardless of background, experience level, or identity. In the spirit of
*Act. Infer. Serve.*, we treat each other with respect and assume good faith.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and email.
- Giving and gracefully accepting feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the Institute's public mission.
- Showing empathy toward other contributors.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberately disruptive behavior, or spam.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — repository issues,
pull requests, discussions, and direct project-related correspondence — and
when an individual is representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the Institute team at **blanket@activeinference.institute**
(also the contact published in [`.well-known/security.txt`](.well-known/security.txt)).

All reports will be reviewed and investigated, and a response will be given that
is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers who do not
follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or
permanent consequences as determined by the Institute.
