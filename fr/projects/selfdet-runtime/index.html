SelfDet Runtime: A Comparative Pilot of Decision Architectures under Conflicting Goals, Constraints, and Delegated Authority

A proposed comparative simulation pilot of decision architectures under conflicting goals, constraints and delegated authority.

Discord invite

SelfDet Runtime is a pre-implementation research proposal by Garegin Miskaryan. The proposed bounded simulation pilot compares LLM-only, rules-only and an integrated controller on task completion, constraint violations, uncertainty and delegated authority. The project seeks methodological reviewers, simulation engineers and independent evaluators. A protocol, matched controls and preregistered evaluation would precede claims of benefit; no implemented system or validated result is claimed. Listing invites voluntary collaboration and does not establish funding, endorsement or an institutional partnership.

Participer

Discuss the proposed scope, review available artifacts and agree on a bounded first milestone with the project facilitator. Progress and open questions can be shared through the Institute's project forms.
