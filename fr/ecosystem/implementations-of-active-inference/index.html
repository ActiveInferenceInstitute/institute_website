About Implementations of Active Inference

In the Active Blockference project, we have curated dozens of Open Source Implementations of Active Inference at this link.

The sub-pages here go into more detail on several different toolkits for applying Active Inference, including in Python (PyMDP), Julia (RxInfer.jl), Matlab (SPM (Statistical Parametric Mapping)), and Prolog (Symbolic Cognitive Robotics).
